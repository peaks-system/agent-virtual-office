The UI uses progressive disclosure. The normal Campaign ledger shows product names, selected Asset Revision, plan/render status, and the two actions. Verified facts, fingerprints, render manifest, and technical output details stay in expandable details. No ranking wording or score is shown. Existing single-product Campaign and Creative Production panels retain their current layout and behavior.
